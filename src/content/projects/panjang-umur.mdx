Panjang Umur is a social platform where users can interact, chat, and challenge each other to do good habits. To encourage the completion of these challenges, a mini economy and shop system was made, where completion rewards currency! These currencies can be used to buy rewards from each other.

## Focus of the project

- Prioritises healthy habits with incentives to make them easier.
- With a friend as well as chat system, Panjang Umur encourages healthy interactions.
