CitaCheck is a citation-finder built for academic writing: paste in a sentence or paragraph and it searches a reference library for passages that match closely enough to cite, ranked by a similarity score you can tune yourself.

## How it works

- Enter the text you want to check against the reference set.
- Optionally filter by source organization.
- Adjust the similarity threshold, where higher means stricter matches while lower surfaces more loosely related references.
- Get back a ranked list of citations to drop straight into the user's paper.

This project grew out of a class project, while I myself was motivated on it by my research work on data hiding and steganography.
