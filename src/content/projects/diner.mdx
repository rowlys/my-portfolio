Diner is a digital document signing platform that changes how documents are managed and signed online. Through its secure digital signing feature, Diner allows document signing requests and sharing, as well as user authentication. Built with a robust backend using Express.js and MongoDB for reliable document storage.

## How it works

- Upload a digital version of your signature.
- Send or receive requests for document signings.
- Sign documents by simply choosing where to put them through an easy to understand UI.
