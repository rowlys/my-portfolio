X-IV is a simple roguelike card game built in Unity. You play by using mathematical operations to the number on the cards you are dealt to get as close as possible to 24. For every number off of 24 you get, your health will be reduced proportionately. And be careful! There are challenges every few levels that hinder you from using certain operations.

## Focus of the prototype

- A tight, single-mechanic gameplay that focuses on creative ways to use the operations.
- A minimal art style to simplify the game's intent.
- To build an inherent 'quick maths' skill within players, helping them solve each levels faster and therefore build engagement
