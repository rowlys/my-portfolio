VARStego is a data-hiding technique built around local variance in a cover image. Rather than modifying pixels uniformly, it embeds payload bits more heavily in high-variance (busy, detailed) regions and backs off in flat ones, keeping the change harder to spot statistically than a naive least-significant-bit scheme.

## What it is

- Grew out of research work on data security and data hiding at NCC Laboratory, alongside a previous technique, BASMEDSECURE.
- Targets the usual steganalysis trade-off: capacity vs. detectability, using local image statistics to decide where it's "safe" to hide more as well as a redundancy-based embedding algorithm.
- Utilizes the Huffman Encoding scheme to minimize required bit embeds.
